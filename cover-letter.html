Cover Letter

This cover letter was written as part of a professional writing course assignment. The goal was to craft a targeted, persuasive letter for a real internship opportunity — in this case, the Automation and Innovation department at the Jacksonville Transportation Authority — demonstrating how academic and practical experiences align with the employer's mission.

4254 Oak Lodge Way
Winter Garden, FL 34787
March 26, 2026
Jacksonville Transportation Authority
100 LaVilla Center Drive
Jacksonville, FL 32204
Dear Recruiting Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Automation and Innovation department at Jacksonville Transportation Authority. I am currently an intended Computer Science major at the University of Central Florida, and I hope to pursue a career in Software Engineering. I am eager to learn practical and innovative skills within the transit industry which can help strengthen my software engineering goals. My strong collaboration skills, desire to learn and lead teams, and analytical thinking ability will allow me to be a strong contributor to a fast-paced innovation team at JTA.

I am particularly drawn to this internship at the Jacksonville Transportation Authority because it offers the opportunity to enhance my technical skill set in software development in an environment that emphasizes smart mobility and automation. I am eager to use my prior experience with team-based software projects and programming coursework to assist the Jacksonville Transportation Authority in its mission to improve transportation accessibility and efficiency.

My experiences with collaborative software development and academic programming have prepared me to be a successful intern. As a student developer, I was responsible for implementing features and troubleshooting code. Through this experience, I learned adaptability and communication, which will be valuable in my work for the Automation and Innovation team. After working on team-based projects, I have developed a strong background in problem solving and am prepared to work with individuals from all cultures and backgrounds.

Additionally, my coursework in programming and data structures has taught me about efficient algorithms and software design. In particular, my experiences with building applications will help me effectively contribute to the Jacksonville Transportation Authority.

As an intern at JTA, I am excited about the opportunity to support innovative transportation solutions. I am confident that I have the technical, communication, and teamwork skills to contribute effectively. I would welcome the chance to discuss my qualifications with you in greater detail. Please do not hesitate to contact me by email at Nathanduong101@gmail.com or by phone at (407) 775-0721. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
Nathan Duong
