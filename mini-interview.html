Mini Interview

This mini interview was completed as part of a professional communication course assignment. The goal was to practice articulating personal background, skills, and career goals in a confident, concise, and professional manner — simulating the experience of a real job or internship interview setting.

Reflection

Recording this mini interview was a valuable exercise in self-presentation and professional communication. Putting my experiences, skills, and goals into spoken words — rather than written form — pushed me to think about how I naturally talk about myself and where I still need to grow in confidence. I found it challenging to be concise while still sounding genuine and complete, especially when discussing technical projects like Hyve in a way that's accessible to a non-technical audience.

Through this assignment, I gained a better appreciation for how much preparation goes into a real interview. Knowing what to say is only part of it — delivery, pacing, and eye contact matter just as much. Going forward, I plan to continue practicing by recording myself, seeking feedback from peers, and attending SASE mock interview workshops to sharpen these skills before applying for internships.
